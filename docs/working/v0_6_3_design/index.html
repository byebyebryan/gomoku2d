About this design study

The shared frame, page compositions and small tools here are a proposal. They are not integrated into the game, and visual acceptance is still pending.

Fonts, controls and all 41 SVGs come from the repository. CSS font paths are rebased for this export; the app is unchanged. Lab figures retain their June 2026 run identities. Review images retain their original 0.6.2 checkpoint, not a new capture date.

Judge the shared navigation, pixel typography, icon comparison, Lab hierarchy and before/after reading. Technical validation of this study does not replace your visual or physical-device review.
